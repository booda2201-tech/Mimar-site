{{ number('overview') }} Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Al Hendal Group ("we", "us", "our") handles personal data in the M3mar mobile application (package {{ packageName }}).

M3mar lets you browse and order construction materials, submit and negotiate requests for quotation (RFQs), and organize materials into collections. This policy covers all of these features. By creating an account or using the app, you acknowledge the practices described here.

We collect only the information you provide to us while using the app. We do not collect data from your device in the background.

credit_card_off

The M3mar app does not collect, process, or store credit or debit card numbers or CVV codes. You are never asked to enter payment card details in the app.

{{ number('sharing') }} How We Share Information

We share personal data only when it is necessary to provide the service:

  • local_shipping Order fulfilment and quotations. When you place an order or submit an RFQ, the details needed to quote, fulfil, and deliver it (your name, phone number, delivery address, and the order or RFQ details) are shared with the supplier or Al Hendal Group team handling it.
  • dns Service providers. Providers that host our servers and store uploaded images process data on our behalf, only on our instructions, and under obligations to protect it.
  • account_balance Legal obligations. When disclosure is required by applicable law or by a valid request from a competent authority.

{{ number('storage-security') }} Storage & Security

  • lock All data exchanged between the app and our servers is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • smartphone On your device, the app keeps only the session data needed to keep you signed in. Your password is never stored on the device, and session data is deleted when you log out.
  • admin_panel_settings Access to personal data on our systems is limited to authorized personnel who need it to operate the service.

{{ number('retention') }} Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service to you.

When you request deletion, we delete your personal data. Records we are legally required to keep, such as transaction records of completed orders, are retained only for the period the law requires and are then deleted.

{{ number('delete-account') }} Delete Your Account & Data

You can remove your data from your device at any time, and you can ask us to erase your account completely.

logout Option 1 — Log out to clear this device

When you log out, the app deletes the session data stored on your device, including your sign-in session and locally cached account information. Your account remains on our servers, so you can sign in again later.

delete_forever Option 2 — Request full account & data erasure
  1. 1 Email {{ email }} from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  2. 2 Include the phone number on your account so we can verify that the request comes from you.
  3. 3 Within 30 days, we permanently delete your account and associated personal data: profile details, delivery addresses, orders, RFQs and negotiations, and material collections with their cover photos.
  4. 4 We confirm by email once the deletion is complete.

Records we are legally required to keep, such as transaction records of completed orders, are retained only for the period the law requires and are then deleted.

forward_to_inbox Email deletion request

{{ number('children') }} Children's Privacy

M3mar is designed for contractors, businesses, and adults purchasing construction materials. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

{{ number('governing-framework') }} Governing Framework

We process personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and its Implementing Regulations, overseen by the Saudi Data & Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

If you believe your personal data has been processed in violation of the PDPL, you may contact us first so we can resolve it, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with SDAIA.

{{ number('changes') }} Changes to This Policy

If we change how the app handles personal data, we will update this policy and its effective date on this page. Where a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you before it takes effect.
